This guide is a textbook for one specific career path: **a software engineer whose core strength is full-stack development and who has moved into applied AI**. That means LLM integration, retrieval (RAG), agents and AI automation, shipped inside real products.

It isn't a machine-learning course. You won't train models from scratch here. The work this guide prepares you for is the layer *around* the model: APIs, data, reliability, security, evaluation, cost and the user interface. Most AI engineering jobs today are that layer.

Read the three lessons below before anything else. They take about half an hour and set up how the other 80-odd lessons are meant to be used.
