Everything in this guide runs on a small set of foundations. You'll use the terminal and git every working day, every feature crosses HTTP, and the two languages that dominate applied AI work are **Python** (backend, data, AI SDKs) and **TypeScript** (frontend and increasingly backend).

This part isn't a replacement for a full language course. It gives you the parts of each topic that matter in production work and in interviews, and points to where to go deeper.

If you already work as an engineer, use the **check-yourself** questions as a diagnostic: read only the lessons where you can't answer them.
