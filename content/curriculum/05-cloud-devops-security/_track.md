Code that only runs on your laptop isn't a product. This part covers what it takes to run a service in production and keep it safe: packaging, hosting, watching it, defending it, and meeting the privacy rules that apply to the data it handles.

Every topic here becomes more important with AI features: model calls are slow and expensive to observe, prompts and documents carry sensitive data, and new attack classes (prompt injection, SSRF through tool calls) appear.
