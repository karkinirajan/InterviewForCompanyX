System design is where Parts 3–5 come together, and where seniority shows most in interviews. There's rarely a single right answer; there are trade-offs made explicit and failure modes named before anyone asks.

Lessons 6.1–6.3 cover the classic material. Lesson 6.4 holds the four AI design drills (D1–D4) used throughout the rest of the guide.
