This is where the guide turns from general engineering to applied AI. Nothing in Parts 1–6 stops being relevant; this part adds one new kind of dependency, a **probabilistic, slow, expensive, rate-limited service that returns text**, and everything you need to use it well.

The theme across the whole part: **let the model choose; let code compute and verify.**
