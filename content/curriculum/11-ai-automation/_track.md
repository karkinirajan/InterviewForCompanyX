Much of today's AI work isn't greenfield chatbots. It's integrating models into existing systems (a CRM, a support desk, an ERP, a clinical workflow) and automating the work between them. That's where full-stack strength becomes an advantage: you already know webhooks, OAuth, queues, idempotency and data modelling.

The final lesson is a capstone: ship one AI feature end to end, using everything in Parts 1–11.
