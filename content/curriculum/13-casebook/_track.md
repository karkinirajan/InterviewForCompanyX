Every principle in this guide is easier to learn from a real system. These case studies are the author's own projects (personal products, client work and subcontracts), written in a standard format you can reuse for your own portfolio.

**Repository numbers are repository metrics, never client outcomes.** Each case study states what's been verified and what hasn't. That honesty is part of the lesson.

Start with [13.1](/13-casebook/01-presenting-projects) for the format and the evidence matrix.
